First public release, as org.jitteros.app.

- A horizon per tracked app, 25 minutes by default. Past 40% of it, scrolls can stall, longer and more often up to a ceiling.
- Opening a tracked app shows a countdown, then a lease of 5, 10 or 15 minutes.
- Locks with no unlock, and a console home screen.
- A setup guide for two permissions (accessibility, usage access) and your home app.
- No network permission. Nothing leaves the device.

Uninstall dev.molasses first; its data does not carry over.
